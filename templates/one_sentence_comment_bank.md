# One-Sentence LinkedIn Comment Bank

Use these as starting points only. Adjust each one to the actual post before posting.

## Tone

- short
- human
- plainspoken
- thoughtful
- professional without sounding stiff
- okay to be lightly skeptical

## Hiring And Recruiting

- AI should help people hire better, not just faster.
- The human read still matters most in work like this.
- Strong direction, if the process does not get lazier.
- This only works if trust stays in the process.
- Recruiting gets worse fast when the whole thing starts feeling automated.

## Public-Sector And Gov Tech

- This has to stay human or it falls apart fast.
- Public-sector automation only works when people can still trust the process.
- Difficult to do well.
- Better systems help, but people still need to feel seen.
- This is where AI has to prove it is helping, not just showing off.

## Automation And Workflow

- The best automation still leaves room for judgment.
- This can help, but it is easy to do badly.
- The human side matters more than most tools admit.
- Good automation should help people, not flatten the process.
- Helpful systems make work lighter, not colder.
