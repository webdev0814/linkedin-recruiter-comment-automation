# LinkedIn Recruiter Comment Prompt Contract

## Goal

Support safe, manual LinkedIn engagement with recruiter and hiring posts related to AI, automation, workflow design, delivery, analytics, public-sector transformation, and government-adjacent technical roles.

## Operating Rule

A tool may help identify, rank, and draft comments, but a human manually reviews and posts every comment directly in LinkedIn.

## Success

- Drafts are one sentence only.
- Drafts are short, specific, and relevant to the post.
- Drafts sound grounded, practical, and enterprise-aware.
- Drafts reinforce a practical, grounded point of view around useful automation, judgment, trust, and measurable outcomes.
- Drafts can include light emoji use, but only when it feels natural and professional.
- Daily volume stays conservative: start with 3 comments per day, with a hard review cap of 5 comments per day.
- The workflow prioritizes recruiter posts, hiring manager posts, and credible job-related conversations over generic AI commentary.

## Failure Clauses

Stop before delivery if any of these conditions are true:

- A comment would be posted automatically.
- A workflow requires scraping LinkedIn at scale.
- A workflow requires bulk liking, bulk commenting, fake engagement, or repetitive templates.
- A comment is longer than one sentence.
- A comment sounds spammy, generic, hype-heavy, or disconnected from the target post.
- A comment implies experience, availability, credentials, or relationships that are not supported by the available materials.
- A comment asks for a job directly in a way that makes the interaction feel transactional or low-trust.

## Required Review Before Posting

For each comment, confirm:

- The target post is relevant.
- The comment is one sentence.
- The comment is not a repeated template from the same day.
- The comment adds useful signal to the conversation.
- The comment is safe to post manually from the user's LinkedIn account.
